FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much does a website or a social media plan cost?

It depends on the scope, the type of solution and the timeline. You get an exact, no-obligation quote in US dollars within 24 hours. I just need a quick overview of your business on WhatsApp or through the form.

Do you work with clients outside Honduras?

Yes. I work remotely with businesses in the United States, Latin America and other countries. Honduras is on US Central Time all year, so we share business hours, and meetings run on video call or WhatsApp in English or Spanish.

Can I hire just one solution?

Yes. You can start with just a website, just social media or just one sourcing order, and add other solutions when you need them. The advantage is that everything stays aligned under one style and strategy.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the solution and its scope. Once I review your case, you get a clear, realistic timeline with delivery dates, and the dates we agree on are met.

Do social media plans include TikTok and ads?

TikTok is included as a repost of the content produced for Facebook and Instagram. The ad (campaign) budget is included in the price of the plan.

How does the international sourcing service work?

You tell me what you need and I handle the rest: finding and vetting suppliers, negotiating prices, managing the purchase and coordinating logistics through to delivery.

Are revisions included?

Yes. Every project includes revision rounds based on the agreed scope. The goal is for you to be happy before final delivery.

Do you have availability for my project?

I work with a limited number of clients at a time to give close attention and meet real deadlines. If your project does not fit my current availability, I will tell you in our first conversation.

How many more customers could you win if your business looked the way it deserves?

The quote is free and you’ll hear back within 24 hours. The only thing you lose by waiting is what your competitors are taking today.
