FAQ

Event planning: frequently asked questions

How far in advance should I contact you?

As early as possible: the best venues and vendors book up early. For large events, ideally start several months ahead; for small events, a few weeks may be enough.

Can you plan an event in Honduras if I live abroad?

Yes. I handle the planning and vendor coordination locally while keeping you updated remotely, so you can organize a corporate event or a celebration in Honduras from anywhere.

Can I hire just part of the event?

Yes. Tell me which part you need (planning, vendors or day-of coordination) and I’ll adapt the proposal.

How do you quote an event?

With the event type, date, city, number of guests and approximate budget, I prepare a custom proposal with no obligation.
