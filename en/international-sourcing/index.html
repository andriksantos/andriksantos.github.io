The problem and the solution

Every mistake in an international purchase eats into your margin

A supplier who doesn’t deliver or a cost you didn’t see coming can turn a good deal into a loss.

What is happening now

  • You don’t know if a supplier is reliable until you’ve paid
  • You buy at list price because you have no negotiating power
  • Freight, customs and taxes show up at the end
  • Delays without information: you don’t know where your cargo is
  • Your team loses hours on quotes and follow-up

What you get

  • Suppliers vetted on quality, price and reliability
  • Negotiation of prices, payment terms and lead times
  • Full landed cost before you buy: product, freight, customs and delivery
  • Cargo tracking from origin to destination
  • Quality control and delivery to your warehouse or store

FAQ

International sourcing: frequently asked questions

How does the sourcing service work?

You tell me what you need and I handle everything: finding and vetting suppliers, negotiating prices, managing the purchase and coordinating logistics through to delivery.

Which countries can you source from?

I work with local and international suppliers. Tell me the product you need and, if you already have a country of origin in mind, I evaluate the best supplier and shipping route options.

Do you handle customs clearance?

Yes. The service includes managing the import and customs clearance into Honduras, coordinating with the right agents through final delivery.

I’m a foreign company. Can you help me buy or distribute in Honduras?

Yes. If you need a local partner to source products, negotiate with suppliers or coordinate logistics in Honduras and Central America, I can help.

How is the service priced?

It depends on the product, volume and complexity of the operation. Before we start you receive a clear proposal with the service fee.

What do you need to buy?

Tell me the product and quantity. I’ll tell you how to bring it in at the best total cost and in what timeframe.
