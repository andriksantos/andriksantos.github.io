FAQ

Web design: frequently asked questions

How much does a website cost?

It depends on the type of site (landing page, business, corporate, catalog, online store or custom) and the features you need. You get an exact, no-obligation quote in US dollars within 24 hours.

How long does it take?

A landing page can be ready in a few days; an online store or a custom solution takes longer. Your proposal includes a timeline with delivery dates.

Will my website show up on Google?

Yes. It is delivered optimized (speed, titles, descriptions and structured data) and registered in Google Search Console. Rankings also depend on competition and content, so I recommend how to keep improving it.

Do I need a logo, copy and photos?

Not necessarily. If you don’t have a logo or graphics, we can solve it with the graphic design service, and I help you write copy with your customers in mind.

Can you build a site in English, Spanish or both?

Yes. I build websites in English, Spanish or bilingual, which is ideal if you sell to both markets.

Do you work with clients outside Honduras?

Yes. I build websites for businesses in the United States, Latin America and other countries, fully remote and on US Central Time.
